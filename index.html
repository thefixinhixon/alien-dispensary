ALIEN DISPENSARY

You are the only human budtender on the station — hired because humans are the one species that doesn't explode, molt, achieve telepathy, or enter a 40-year hibernation from a 10 mg gummy. Ten customers. One counter. A stamp with your name on it.

HOW A SHIFT WORKS

  • Read the request. Customers describe their problems terribly. That's the job.
  • Check the documents. Galactic ID, permit, and the Species Tolerance Registry. Something is usually off.
  • Pick a product from the shelf that matches their need, their species, and the law — or don't sell at all.
  • Stamp it. APPROVED sells your selected product. DENIED sends them away. Both can be right. Both can end your career.

DAY 1 RULES — THE SHORT VERSION

  • Adult age is different for every species. A 5-year-old Klarg is a grown taxpayer. An 87-year-old Nebula Ent is a child.
  • Medical-only products need a valid, unexpired medical permit.
  • The Registry flags hard limits — THC caps, edible dose caps, allergens. Registry beats customer confidence.
  • 3 citations from the ICD (Intergalactic Compliance Division) and you're fired. The stamp giveth, the stamp taketh away.

SCORING

  • Perfect match: sale + tip. Wrong-but-legal product: sale, no tip, side-eye.
  • Correct denial: +15₡ compliance bonus. Wrongful denial: a 1-star review with your name in it.
  • Illegal sale: citation. Yes, even if they were really nice about it.

FROM YOUR MANAGER, DEB

  • "Welcome aboard. The handbook button is right under Clock In — and there's another one on the counter all shift. Read it, it's the whole job."
  • "Do NOT upsell the loyalty program to anyone who is actively molting."
  • "If someone says 'my cousin's a budtender on Colorado Prime,' that is not a medical permit."

    Standing rules

    • Medical-only products (tagged on the shelf) require a medical permit that is valid today. "It expired yesterday" is expired.
    • Registry flags — THC caps, dose caps, allergens — are hard law, not suggestions. The customer's confidence is not evidence.
    • Recreational permit covers everything not tagged medical-only. No permit at all = no sale, full stop.
    • Three ICD citations in a shift and you're fired on the spot. Deb will not cry. Deb never cries.
